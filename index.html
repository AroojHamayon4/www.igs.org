Welcome to IGS

The "Intellectuals of Global System" (IGS) is an independent academic platform focusing on the intersection of international relations theory, global governance, normative systems, and the politics of inclusion. Our core objective is to explore and propose frameworks for multilateralism, diplomatic equilibrium, and cosmopolitan ideals in an increasingly multipolar world. IGS seeks to serve as a common ground for discourses on sovereignty, power structures, development disparities, and collective security mechanisms — engaging with both traditional and non-traditional security paradigms.
